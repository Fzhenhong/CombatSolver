# CombatSolver 0.48.2

## 简体中文

相对于0.48.1的更新：

- 修复采用计算中的路线、继续已有路线和部分遗物状态导致的计算失败；药水使用继续遵守当前策略、保护设置和使用配额。
- 修复回合准备阶段生成手牌，以及卡牌强制结束回合后选牌的预测与执行错误。
- 修复同名卡牌选牌顺序、复制牌入场费用与满手牌时的返手次序，减少由此触发的路线中止和计划外重算。
- 修复群体施加减益后抽牌与自动出牌的结算顺序，以及毒素触发伤害与敌方格挡的结算顺序。
- 修复复制减益时误带上本次攻击新增效果，以及同次出牌多次获得格挡时的预测偏差。
- 修复生成牌、闪电和出牌历史在实际执行推进后被重复计入预测的问题，改善路线伤害及回合结束风险判断。
- 修复仪式与临时力量在回合末的结算顺序；修复失衡攻击被完全格挡后敌人眩晕意图的预测。
- 修复复活后的受伤量比较；三选一自动选择会等待页面接受正确卡牌，页面失配时保留原生选择供玩家处理。

## English

Changes since 0.48.1:

- Fixed calculation failures when adopting an in-progress route, continuing a route, or capturing certain relic states. Potion use follows the current policy, protection settings and usage quota.
- Fixed predicted and executed card choices involving cards generated during turn preparation and cards that force the turn to end.
- Fixed selection order for identical cards, costs of newly created copies, and return-to-hand order with a full hand, reducing interrupted execution and unexpected recalculations.
- Fixed the order of card draws and automatic plays triggered by debuffs applied to multiple enemies, and the order of poison-triggered damage relative to enemy block.
- Fixed debuff copying that incorrectly included effects created by the current attack, and block predictions when one card grants block multiple times.
- Fixed generated-card, Lightning-channel and card-play history being counted again after live execution advanced, improving damage predictions and end-of-turn risk checks.
- Fixed end-of-turn ordering between Ritual and temporary Strength, and predicted Stunned intents after fully blocked Imbalanced attacks.
- Fixed damage-loss comparisons after revival. Automatic choose-a-card selection now waits for the native page to accept the intended card; a mismatched page remains available for manual selection.
