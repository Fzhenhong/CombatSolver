# CombatSolver 0.48.2

## 简体中文

相对于 0.48.1 的更新：

- 修复采用计算中的路线和部分遗物状态导致的计算失败；药水使用遵守当前策略、保护设置和使用配额。
- 修复卡牌强制结束回合后，下一回合选牌的预测与执行错误。
- 修复同名卡牌选牌顺序、复制牌入场费用与满手牌时的返手次序，减少由此触发的路线中止和计划外重算。
- 修复群体施加减益后抽牌与自动出牌的结算顺序，以及毒素触发伤害与敌方格挡的结算顺序。
- 修复复制减益时误带上本次攻击新增效果，以及同次出牌多次获得格挡时的预测偏差。
- 修复生成牌、闪电和出牌历史在实际执行推进后被重复计入预测的问题，改善路线伤害及回合结束风险判断。
- 修复仪式与临时力量在回合末的结算顺序；修复失衡攻击被完全格挡后敌人眩晕意图的预测。
- 修复复活后的受伤量比较；三选一自动选择会等待页面接受正确卡牌，页面失配时保留原生选择供玩家处理。
- 感谢 [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly) 提交的 [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203)：修正获得金币、提高最大生命及连带回血的预测，按实际所得评估收益，并正确考虑相关遗物的回血机会。
- 感谢 [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly) 提交的 [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203)：修复部分战斗遗漏胜利路线，以及金纸相关状态导致路线选择错误的问题。
- 感谢 [yM7-1](https://github.com/yM7-1) 提交的 [PR #204](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/204)：修复战斗提前结束、或卡牌强制结束回合后，继续搜索已有路线时的计算失败。
- 感谢 [yM7-1](https://github.com/yM7-1) 提交的 [PR #204](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/204)：修复遗物在首回合抽牌前生成手牌时，路线与实际手牌不一致的问题。
- 感谢 [yM7-1](https://github.com/yM7-1) 提交的 [PR #204](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/204)：修复格挡倍率大量叠加时的计算失败。
- 感谢 [yM7-1](https://github.com/yM7-1) 提交的 [PR #204](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/204)：补齐长战斗计算的内存保护，根据可用内存设置计算上限。

## English

Changes since 0.48.1:

- Fixed calculation failures when adopting an in-progress route or capturing certain relic states. Potion use follows the current policy, protection settings and usage quota.
- Fixed predicted and executed card choices at the start of the next turn after a card forces the current turn to end.
- Fixed selection order for identical cards, costs of newly created copies, and return-to-hand order with a full hand, reducing interrupted execution and unexpected recalculations.
- Fixed the order of card draws and automatic plays triggered by debuffs applied to multiple enemies, and the order of poison-triggered damage relative to enemy block.
- Fixed debuff copying that incorrectly included effects created by the current attack, and block predictions when one card grants block multiple times.
- Fixed generated-card, Lightning-channel and card-play history being counted again after live execution advanced, improving damage predictions and end-of-turn risk checks.
- Fixed end-of-turn ordering between Ritual and temporary Strength, and predicted Stunned intents after fully blocked Imbalanced attacks.
- Fixed damage-loss comparisons after revival. Automatic choose-a-card selection now waits for the native page to accept the intended card; a mismatched page remains available for manual selection.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Corrected predictions for gold gains, maximum HP gains and their healing effects. Route rewards now use the actual gains, and healing opportunities from related relics are properly considered.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed missed winning routes in some fights and incorrect route selection caused by Joss Paper state.
- [PR #204](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/204) by [yM7-1](https://github.com/yM7-1): Fixed calculation failures when continuing a route after combat ends early or a card forces the turn to end.
- [PR #204](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/204) by [yM7-1](https://github.com/yM7-1): Fixed route and hand mismatches when relics generate cards before the opening draw.
- [PR #204](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/204) by [yM7-1](https://github.com/yM7-1): Fixed calculation failures when block multipliers accumulate to very high values.
- [PR #204](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/204) by [yM7-1](https://github.com/yM7-1): Added memory protection for long combat searches, setting calculation limits according to available memory.
