# CombatSolver 0.48.1

## 简体中文

- 修正手动喝药后的“更优世界线”提示：每多用一瓶药，预计总战损至少下降9 HP才提示；原路线已经计划使用的药不会重复计入成本。问题反馈、日志和后台同时展示多用药瓶数及折算后的优化战损。
- 修复部分自动出牌、生成牌与回合末消耗的预测偏差，改善倾泻配合无尽陀螺时的选牌，以及金纸配合生成牌时的消耗处理。
- 修复压缩产生的牌顺序与原版不一致，以及子弹时间后临时星能费用残留导致的重算。

## English

- Corrected the “better worldline” feedback after manual potion use: each additional potion must save at least 9 projected HP. Potions already planned by the original route are counted once. Reports, logs and the dashboard show additional potion use and the HP improvement after accounting for potion cost.
- Fixed prediction differences in automatic plays, generated cards and end-of-turn exhaustion, including Cascade with Unceasing Top and Joss Paper with generated cards.
- Fixed the order of cards created by Compact and expired temporary Star costs after Bullet Time that could cause recalculation.
