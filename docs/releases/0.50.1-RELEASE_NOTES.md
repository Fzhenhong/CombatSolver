# CombatSolver 0.50.1

## 简体中文

- 修复智能药水在只节省少量生命时仍消耗多瓶药水的问题。继续优化路线时也会计入药水价值，保留必要保命、强制使用与战后换药抵扣的策略。
- 感谢 [shun-tong](https://github.com/shun-tong) 提交的 [PR #213](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/213)：改善长战斗中能力牌与用药后的路线优化，强制用药后也能从仍可行动的回合继续寻找更好路线。
- 感谢 [link20031019](https://github.com/link20031019) 提交的 [PR #226](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/226)：Ctrl+F9 隐藏面板后，界面刷新和战斗重置会保持隐藏，再次按键恢复显示。
- 感谢 [tianyilt](https://github.com/tianyilt) 提交的 [PR #224](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/224)：改善手牌上限发生变化时的路线复用判断。

## English

- Fixed Smart potion choices spending multiple potions for very small HP savings. Continued route optimization now accounts for potion value, while preserving necessary survival uses, Force directives, and replacement credit for post-combat potion rewards.
- [PR #213](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/213) by [shun-tong](https://github.com/shun-tong): Improved route optimization after playing powers and using potions in long battles. Searches can continue from playable turn boundaries after forced potion use.
- [PR #226](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/226) by [link20031019](https://github.com/link20031019): Panels hidden with Ctrl+F9 stay hidden across UI refreshes and combat resets. Press the shortcut again to show them.
- [PR #224](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/224) by [tianyilt](https://github.com/tianyilt): Improved route reuse checks when the hand-size limit changes.
